01 / Meet SoulTime

A little vintage.
A lot of soul.

Four seasoned musicians. Four voices. An irresistible mix of soul, funk, disco, Motown, and familiar favorites.

Female-fronted and based in Central Florida, SoulTime brings a driving dance beat and a professional sound to weddings, private celebrations, corporate events, and club stages.

02 / Behind the soul

Meet your band.

Individual stories.
A shared love of the groove.

Lead vocals / Keys

Cherry Vicidomine

Behind the voice +

A New York native with a versatile voice shaped by pop, R&B, soul, and ballads. Her influences include Whitney Houston, Aretha Franklin, and Linda Ronstadt.

Guitar / Vocals

Dave DePrest

Behind the guitar +

A lifelong guitarist whose musical path has taken him through rock, jazz, folk, reggae, blues, and country. He brings that range to SoulTime’s funky guitar sound.

Bass / Vocals

Pete Balciunas

Behind the bass +

A bassist with roots in Northwest Indiana and jazz studies in Chicago. His background spans performance, recording, production, and live audio.

Drums / Vocals

Vinny Vicidomine

Behind the beat +

A Long Island drummer with a love of soul, funk, and Motown. His solid backbeat and smooth groove keep the dance floor moving.
