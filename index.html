Meet SoulTime

A little vintage.
A lot of soul.

SoulTime brings together four experienced musicians for an energetic dance-band experience. Led by a female vocalist, with every member contributing vocals, the group has entertained Central Florida audiences for about a decade.

Their repertoire celebrates familiar favorites, from soul, funk, disco, and Motown to newer hits, with a special affection for vintage sounds. Recognizable songs and a strong dance groove keep the energy moving from one crowd favorite to the next.

From corporate gatherings and fairs to after-parties, private celebrations, weddings, and club appearances, SoulTime pairs polished sound with a professional approach. The band can also consider travel when the event is a good fit.

Ready for a night of dancing, familiar hits, and good company? SoulTime brings the music and the energy to turn your occasion into a party.

The Band

Individual stories.
A shared love of the groove.

Lead vocals / Keys

Cherry Vicidomine

Behind the voice

Cherry began singing at twelve on Long Island. Her career includes performances at New York’s Rainbow Room and Waldorf Astoria, followed by leading Cherry Pie and Cross Town Rhythm. Her versatile style draws on Whitney Houston, Aretha Franklin, and Linda Ronstadt.

Drums / Vocals

Vinny Vicidomine

Behind the beat

Vinny started drumming at fourteen with encouragement from his brother Anthony. His Long Island bands included The Reign, ESP, Blue Ice, and GoodFellas. A drummer and vocalist, he brings a steady groove and a love of soul, funk, and Motown to SoulTime.
